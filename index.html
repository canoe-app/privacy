Canoe

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Summary

Canoe has no user accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. We do not receive your classroom information through your use of the app. It stays on your device unless you enable iCloud Sync or choose to export or share it. If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever information you include.

Information you enter

Teachers may enter classroom information such as student names, characters, attendance, points, teams, jobs, rewards and classroom records. This information is stored locally on the device running Canoe, is used only to provide Canoe’s classroom features and is never sent to Canoe or to any third party.

Sample class

The sample class is made up of fictional students created on your device. Nothing you do in it is saved, synced, backed up or exported, and it never changes your own class.

iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is off unless you turn it on in Settings. When it’s on, your class is stored in the private iCloud database of your own Apple Account, so it stays the same across your devices. Classroom information is end-to-end encrypted before it leaves your device. Neither Canoe nor Apple can read it, and Canoe cannot access your iCloud data. Turning sync off stops syncing and keeps the class on your device. You can delete Canoe’s iCloud data at any time in your device’s iCloud settings.

Please check your school’s policies before storing student information in a personal iCloud account.

Smart Display

Smart Display shows classroom information, such as student names, characters and points, on a TV or projector you connect. Your device sends it directly to that display using AirPlay or a cable. Canoe does not receive it.

Settings, editors, Teacher Peek and stats are never shown on the big screen. If you choose Mirror My Screen instead, the display shows everything on your screen. Anyone in the room can see the display, so disconnect it before opening anything private.

Backups

If you export a classroom backup, Canoe creates a file and you choose where to save or share it. Canoe does not receive that file. The file is not encrypted, so anyone who has it can read the classroom information inside. You’re responsible for choosing a secure place to keep it and for following your school’s privacy rules.

If you restore from a backup, Canoe reads the file you choose and stores its contents on your device. The file isn’t sent anywhere.

Device backups, such as iCloud or computer backups set up by the device owner, may include Canoe’s app data. Apple’s privacy policy and your device settings govern those backups.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Canoe can alert you when a classroom timer ends. These notifications are scheduled on your device. No information is sent to Canoe.

Purchases

Canoe is offered as a monthly or yearly subscription. Apple processes all subscriptions and payments, including free trials. Canoe uses Apple’s StoreKit purchase information only to check whether your subscription is active and which plan you have. Canoe never receives or stores your payment details.

Feedback and support

If you send feedback from within Canoe, an email draft opens containing your feedback type, your app version and anything you choose to write. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. We use emails only to reply to you and to improve Canoe, and we don’t share them with anyone.

Keeping and deleting information

Classroom information stays on your device until you remove it in the app, start a new class or delete Canoe. Canoe also keeps a few automatic copies of your class on this device for up to 14 days, used only to repair your class if its file is ever damaged. Starting a new empty class removes them. They never leave your device, except in device backups you set up. Deleting Canoe removes its local app data. If iCloud Sync is on, your changes, including deletions, also apply to the copy in your iCloud account. That copy stays until you delete it in your iCloud settings. Exported backup files and device backups are separate. You need to delete them from wherever they’re stored.

Children’s information

Canoe is meant for educators, not for children to use directly. Teachers and schools are responsible for making sure their use of student information follows their own policies and applicable law. Where appropriate, consider using first names or nicknames.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date at the top and the version shown in the app.
